How to play

Tap a tube, then tap another to pour. You can pour onto the same color or into an empty tube, as long as there is room.

Finish when every tube holds a single color.

Mystery levels hide the layers under the top one. They are revealed as you uncover them.

Tight levels give you only one spare tube. Limited levels cap your moves. Later levels also limit undos.

Every level is checked to be solvable. Beat par for 3 stars.
